CLIENT QUESTIONS

Know what to expect.
Before we begin.

Straight answers to the questions that matter when you choose a digital partner.

Do I need to know exactly what I need?

No. Start with your business, your customers and what is getting in the way. We can help turn that into a useful brief.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current setup before recommending targeted changes or a rebuild. Access, platform limitations and existing licences are considered in the scope.

How do you price projects?

Work is quoted against an agreed scope. Page count, content, integrations and complexity affect the quote. Hosting, ad spend, licences and ongoing services are identified separately.

What if I need something extra during the project?

We explain the effect on cost and timing before adding work outside the agreed scope. That keeps decisions deliberate and avoids surprise additions.

What do you need from my business?

Usually a point of contact, an overview of your services, brand assets, content and access to relevant systems. We identify the specific requirements before the project starts.

How long will it take?

The timeline depends on scope, access, content readiness and review time. Your proposal includes a planned schedule and the milestones that need your input.

Will I have access to my website and accounts?

Access, ownership, hosting and third-party licences are documented in your proposal. We plan a practical handover so you understand how your project is operated.

What happens after launch?

We agree the handover and any post-launch support in advance. Ongoing website care, campaign management and improvements can be scoped separately.

Is Google advertising spend included?

Ad spend is separate from agency fees unless the proposal explicitly states otherwise. Reporting should distinguish the cost of media from the cost of managing it.

Can you guarantee rankings, leads or sales?

No. Search visibility and commercial results depend on competition, budget, the offer and the wider customer experience. We agree what to measure and use that evidence to make improvements.

Does my business need AI?

Not necessarily. A clearer form or a simple integration may solve the problem more reliably. We recommend AI where it serves a defined task, with human review where appropriate.

Do you create ads using AI?

Yes. AI can support ideas, copy variations and creative production within an agreed brief. Customer-facing material is reviewed for accuracy, brand fit and usage rights before approval.

Is ChatGPT advertising the same as using AI to create an ad?

No. ChatGPT advertising is paid placement on that platform. AI-assisted creative is a production method that can support ads on other channels. We define both clearly in the scope.
