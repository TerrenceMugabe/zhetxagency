What to prepare before you commission a website. By Zhetx Agency · 25 October 2026

You do not need to arrive with a technical specification. You do need a clear picture of your business and what the website should help people do.

1. Explain who you serve.

Describe the customers you want, the areas you operate in and the services or products they buy. Share the questions customers ask before they are ready to enquire.

2. Pick the main action.

Should a visitor request a quote, buy a product, book a visit or start a conversation? Agree the primary action and the information you need when it happens.

3. Gather the useful material.

Bring your logo files, service descriptions, product information, approved photos and genuine project examples. Identify who can approve wording and who owns the rights to any supplied material.

4. Explain the work behind the form.

Where should enquiries go? Who receives them? Which tools are involved? The website needs to fit the process that follows the customer’s action.

5. Make the handover part of the brief.

Discuss domain access, hosting, source files, account ownership, licences and future updates before work starts. A clear proposal should describe what is included and what remains an ongoing cost.

Start with what you know.

A short explanation of your business and priorities is enough for an initial conversation.
